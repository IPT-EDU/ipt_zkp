# Zero Knowledge Proofs

The course "Modern Zero-Knowledge Proof Protocols" is dedicated to the so-called ZK protocols, which are the foundation of privacy in distributed systems and private computing. They allow for the creation of an irrefutable proof of knowledge of certain information that must be kept secret (for example, proving you are of legal age based on your passport data without revealing it). The course covers the theoretical aspects of constructing ZK protocols, security proofs, and their practical applications.
